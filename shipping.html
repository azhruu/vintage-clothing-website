Shipping & Returns

Clear delivery, return, and care expectations for one-of-one vintage garments.

Shipping

Orders ship from the studio within 1 to 3 business days. Standard tracked shipping is free on orders over $250 in this demo experience.

Returns

Vintage items may be returned within 14 days in their original shipped condition. Because each piece is unique, exchanges depend on current inventory.

Condition

Signs of age are part of vintage character. We disclose notable wear, repairs, distressing, and fit considerations on each product page.
