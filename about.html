Origin story

A boutique archive for modern dressers.

We believe clothing should carry memory. Our pieces are selected for their shape, texture, history, and ability to become part of a new story.

The result is a slower, more intentional way to shop. Each garment is photographed with mood, measured with precision, and described honestly.

Curation process

Selected for silhouette, surface, and soul.

We inspect construction, fabric hand, fading, repairs, hardware, and fit. Condition is documented clearly, and every size is treated as a measurement story instead of a label guess.
